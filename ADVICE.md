# How to Ace Your Academic Website

## 1. Minimalist is King
You are already doing this well. Academic sites should be content-first. Avoid clutter. Your choice of "Paper" theme is excellent.

## 2. High-Quality Content over Quantity
- **Publications**: Ensure every paper has a PDF link and a clean citation. This is the #1 thing researchers look for.
- **Blog Posts**: Don't just post updates. Write about *ideas*, *tutorials*, or *summaries* of your research. A single high-quality explanation of a complex topic is worth 100 "I updated my site" posts.

## 3. SEO & Connectivity
- **Google Scholar**: Essential. You added the link, which is great.
- **Twitter/X**: Engage with the AI community.
- **GitHub**: Make sure your `github` link is active and your pinned repositories are relevant to your research interests (ML, PINNs, Robotics).

## 4. The "About" Section
Your homepage is your elevator pitch.
- Keep the "Senior Undergraduate @ IIT Delhi" tag. It establishes context immediately.
- The extended bio on the homepage is good. Keep it up to date.

## 5. Performance
Static sites like Hugo are fast. Keep image sizes optimized (avatar is perfect now). Don't add heavy analytics scripts unless necessary.

## 6. Future Proofing
- **Domain**: Buy `yourname.com` or `yourname.io` if available.
- **Email**: `mailto` links are good, but consider a contact form if spam becomes an issue (though for personal academic sites, direct email is standard).
