Hi! I'm Lokesh, an incoming M.S. in Data Science student at **New York University (NYU CDS)** and a recent **Engineering Physics** graduate from the **Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Delhi**.

I'm interested in **machine learning**, **natural language processing**, and **AI for Science**. My experiences in research and industry have allowed me to work on problems spanning large language models, scientific machine learning, and cloud infrastructure—shaping the way I think about building intelligent systems.
